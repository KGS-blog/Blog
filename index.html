World adalah harga acuan internasional (C-Market) yang dikonversi ke Rupiah per kg. Ini harga “papan” di New York — bukan harga di kebun Indonesia.

+Diff (Diferensial) adalah tambahan (atau pengurangan, kalau negatif) di atas harga acuan, sesuai kualitas dan asal. Diff +20¢ berarti kopi ini diperdagangkan 20 sen di atas C-Market.

Diff sangat tergantung kondisi — tidak ada angka baku. Pembaca dapat mengeditnya di Kalkulator; sesuaikan Diff dengan kondisi pengolahan (processing) Anda: metode (washed, natural, wet-hull, honey), skor cupping, defect & moisture, asal, ukuran lot, sertifikasi, dan relasi dengan buyer. Gambaran umum: robusta komersial +$0.15–0.30, arabika regional +$0.50–1.20, specialty 86+ +$1.50–2.50 — dan bisa negatif untuk grade bawah atau lot besar tanpa diferensiasi.

Contoh hitung: C-Market 274.90¢ + Diff 20¢ = 294.90¢/lb → (294.90 ÷ 100) ÷ 0.4536 = $6.50/kg → × kurs 17.836 ≈ Rp116.000/kg. Angka ini yang tampil di kotak “+Diff” widget.

Cherry adalah estimasi matematis harga di tingkat petani dalam bentuk cherry/gabah, memakai rasio konversi (5:1 = ±5 kg cherry menjadi 1 kg green bean). Bukan harga riil kebun — harga sebenarnya ditentukan pasar lokal, kadar air, dan negosiasi.

Specialty Grade = C-Market + premium berdasarkan skor cupping (Q-Grade).

Semua angka adalah benchmark untuk menilai wajar atau tidaknya sebuah harga (over/under-value) — bukan pengganti quotation resmi.

World is the international benchmark price (C-Market) converted to IDR per kg — the New York “board” price, not the farm-gate price in Indonesia.

+Diff (Differential) is the premium (or discount, if negative) over the benchmark, reflecting quality and origin. +20¢ means this coffee trades 20 cents above the C-Market.

Diff is highly condition-dependent — there is no fixed number. You can edit it in the Calculator; adjust Diff to your processing conditions: method (washed, natural, wet-hull, honey), cupping score, defect & moisture, origin, lot size, certification, and buyer relationship. Rough guide: commercial robusta +$0.15–0.30, regional arabica +$0.50–1.20, specialty 86+ +$1.50–2.50 — and it can go negative for low grades or large undifferentiated lots.

Worked example: C-Market 274.90¢ + Diff 20¢ = 294.90¢/lb → (294.90 ÷ 100) ÷ 0.4536 = $6.50/kg → × rate 17,836 ≈ IDR 116,000/kg. That is the figure shown in the widget’s “+Diff” tile.

Cherry is a mathematical estimate of the farm-gate price in cherry/parchment form, using a conversion ratio (5:1 = ±5 kg cherry per 1 kg green bean). It is not the real farm price — actual prices are set by local markets, moisture content, and negotiation.

Specialty Grade = C-Market + premium by cupping score (Q-Grade).

All figures are benchmarks for judging whether a price is fair (over/under-value) — not a substitute for an official quotation.

Apa yang mempengaruhi pergerakan ini:
  • Pasokan: panen Brasil (arabika) & Vietnam (robusta), cuaca (frost Minas Gerais, kekeringan Central Highlands), siklus on-year/off-year.
  • Permintaan: konsumsi global, stok roaster, substitusi arabika↔robusta saat selisih harga melebar.
  • Makro & geopolitik: kekuatan USD, kurs IDR (untuk harga Rupiah), konflik & freight (Laut Merah), tarif dagang.
  • Spekulasi: posisi dana berjangka (COT) memperbesar volatilitas jangka pendek.
  • Kebijakan: EUDR, aturan ekspor negara produsen, perubahan regulasi impor.
What drives these movements:
  • Supply: Brazil (arabica) & Vietnam (robusta) harvests, weather (Minas Gerais frost, Central Highlands drought), on/off-year cycles.
  • Demand: global consumption, roaster stocks, arabica↔robusta substitution when the price gap widens.
  • Macro & geopolitics: USD strength, IDR exchange rate (for IDR prices), conflict & freight (Red Sea), trade tariffs.
  • Speculation: futures fund positioning (COT) amplifies short-term volatility.
  • Policy: EUDR, producer-country export rules, import regulation changes.
